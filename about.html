About Game Galaxy

An online retail game store built around a bright, dark, colorful gaming theme.

Store Concept

Game Galaxy is an online retail game store that sells game systems, video games, controllers, chargers, merchandise, and gift cards.

Target Audience

The store is aimed at people who like to play video games, people who are game designers, and people who want to get into gaming.

Brand Theme

The design uses the proposal's bright, dark, and colorful identity, with a red background accent, dark foregrounds, purple middle-ground elements, silver supporting text, and deep navy areas.

Store Experience

The website lets customers move from the homepage to products, add items to a shopping cart, review the cart, enter shipping and purchase information, apply a coupon, and complete a demo purchase.
